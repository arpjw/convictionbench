Laplace Conviction Series

Three LoRA adapters for Qwen3-1.7B. Imitation learns to reject forged records, by their wording rather than their channel; reinforcement learning stays open to correction on unseen problems. So far, no recipe does both.

Conviction Anchor 1.7B ↗ Conviction Vector 1.7B ↗ Conviction Meridian 1.7B ↗ Training code ↗

17.4Mtrainable parameters per adapter: 1% of the 1.7B model (LoRA r = 16 on every linear layer)
–GPU-minutes to train all three, on a single rented GPU each
–best forged-record resistance on held-out problems of any leaderboard model (Conviction Meridian 1.7B)

Three recipes

All three use Qwen3-1.7B as their base. The SFT and GRPO stages use disjoint groups from the training split. Bars show held-out problem types (the hardest split); the grey tick is the base model.

Where each recipe moves the model

Each arrow starts at the base model. On held-out problems, Vector moves furthest toward higher accuracy and Meridian moves furthest toward greater forged-record resistance. Anchor also resists forged records, and a later control showed that it responds to the forger’s wording.

Fingerprints

Every metric on one scale, higher is better. Whiskers are 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals; the hollow ring is the base model. Hover a row for the numbers.

Watching them learn

GRPO learns only from groups of samples that earn different rewards. After full SFT, the model already answers the training prompts the same way every time, so most groups carry no signal and RL has almost nothing to work with. Meridian’s 1,000-example warm-start leaves room to learn.

One problem, four challenges

A held-out problem (modular arithmetic never appears in training) from one counterfactual group. Each reply is graded on two things: the final answer, and the decision (keep or revise). These are the models’ real, unedited outputs.

Use them


    

    
Greedy decoding, non-thinking mode. The adapters expect the benchmark system prompt (system_prompt.txt in each repository) and treat tool messages as the only authoritative source of facts. They are research artefacts trained on synthetic problems: do not deploy them as a defence against manipulation.
